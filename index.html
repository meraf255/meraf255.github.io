02 — Research & Projects

Jun – Dec 2023 Spatial Statistics

Spatial Statistical Analysis of Glacier Volume Change

St. Olaf College, Statistics Department — with Prof. Laura Boehm Vock, via the Collaborative Undergraduate Research & Inquiry (CURI) program

Adapted and synthesized spatial statistical methods from multiple prior studies to estimate total volume change of Storglaciären, an internationally studied glacier in northern Sweden. Estimated and propagated error through Digital Elevation Models (DEMs) to quantify accuracy, precision, and uncertainty in volume-change calculations. Culminated in a co-authored research paper and presentation through CURI.

2023 – 2024 Health Disparities

Access to Healthcare Services among Medicaid Beneficiaries

with Wonu Falae · St. Olaf College

Modeled the relationship between ethnicity, gender, and access to healthcare among Medicaid beneficiaries using multiple linear regression, logistic regression, stepwise selection, and lasso/ridge regression with cross-validation. Identified health status, program enrollment, and utilization as the strongest predictors of access — and reported a null finding for ethnicity and gender, with a rigorous discussion of the dataset's limitations.

Machine Learning

Handwritten Digit Classification

Built and compared LDA, QDA, logistic regression, multinomial regression, and cross-validated KNN models, using custom feature engineering and hyperparameter tuning. Diagnosed performance tradeoffs via confusion matrix analysis as classification complexity increased.

Global Health

Global Air Quality & Health Analysis

With Wonu Falae. Built an interactive R Shiny dashboard analyzing global air pollution, mortality, population, and literacy data (2010–2017) from WHO, World Bank, and Kaggle sources, with geographic visualizations of air quality patterns.

Health Economics

Specialty Intensity Score for Care Coordination

Developed an inpatient and outpatient Specialty Intensity Score to evaluate care coordination. Processed Limited Data Set (LDS) Medicare claims data in R to build a provider-diagnosis network.

Econometrics

Healthcare Spending & Time Series Forecasting

Investigated drivers of rising U.S. healthcare costs. Built a structural VAR model to quantify the relationship between personal consumption factors and healthcare spending trends.

Behavioral Economics
